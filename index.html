Features

How to Use
01
Open the Checklist
Click "Open Checklist" above. The tool runs entirely in your browser — no account or install required.
02
Work Through Each Clause
Expand clauses using the navigation pills or the section headers. Click items to mark them complete.
03
Document Gaps & Actions
Use the comment icon (✎) on each item to record gaps, non-conformities, or action references.
04
Export Your Remarks
Use the "Export Remarks" button to download a structured text file of all your notes for audit packs or management reports.
